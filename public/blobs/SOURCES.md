# Where the images went

This release carries no image bytes. Every picture on the site is generated by the site itself: the canvas
pattern labs and the word labs draw their art at runtime, so nothing here has to be shipped.

The images that used to sit in this folder were removed. The phone screenshots showed a real person's life (a
settings screen with a name, an inbox with real senders and invoices, a shopping history with prices and order
numbers, a home screen with a town name and a thermostat and energy readings, a chat log). The two landscape
photos had no licence, and one of them carried a third party's camera EXIF.

Nothing third-party and nothing personal ships in this release.
