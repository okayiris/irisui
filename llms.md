# For LLMs

This system is 74 parts, 9 chapters and 31 tokens.
Read /irisui/llms.txt first: it is the map, one line per part. Then the page of the part you are about to use
(/irisui/components/<id>.md), and the chapter of the surface you are building for (/irisui/chapters/<id>.md). Every
value comes from the tokens: load /irisui/ds/tokens.css and use the variables.
