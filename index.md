# Iris UI

Every Iris part, foundation and layout, with live previews, exact tokens and the rules that hold them. For people and for LLMs.

## Foundations

- `/irisui/foundations/meaning.md` — Meaning: what may go where: Every drawing says something: an orb is Iris, light is work, her hand is personal.
- `/irisui/foundations/colour.md` — Colour: Near-black, one accent, fifteen topics.
- `/irisui/foundations/type.md` — Type: The system stack, eight named sizes, mono for labels.
- `/irisui/foundations/shape.md` — Shape: Glass, 18px cards, pills.
- `/irisui/foundations/elevation.md` — Elevation: No shadows: light on the edge.
- `/irisui/foundations/motion.md` — Motion: Two durations, one curve, her ring.
- `/irisui/foundations/interaction.md` — Interaction: Press, swipe, sheets, the done moment, haptics: every hand gets an answer.
- `/irisui/foundations/state.md` — State: Hover, press, focus, disabled, busy.
- `/irisui/foundations/spacing.md` — Spacing: 12 and 16, 8 inside.
- `/irisui/foundations/layout.md` — Layout: Phone, window, wide: what changes.
- `/irisui/foundations/icons.md` — Icons: Line icons, 24px, from the system.
- `/irisui/foundations/accessibility.md` — Accessibility: what it holds, and what it owes: Contrast, targets, motion, language.

## Patterns

- `/irisui/patterns/phone.md` — A phone screen: One answer, one accent, one mark.
- `/irisui/patterns/window.md` — A window she opens: Her window in the web app.
- `/irisui/patterns/loop.md` — A loop: One thing that comes back, in phases.
- `/irisui/patterns/apps.md` — An app she builds: Sidebar, tabs, list-detail, board, flow, document, table.
- `/irisui/patterns/search.md` — Search and finding: Where it sits, what it does while it waits.
- `/irisui/patterns/settings.md` — Settings and the You page: Rows, values, danger at the bottom.
- `/irisui/patterns/loading.md` — Loading and empty: Never an empty black screen.
- `/irisui/patterns/errors.md` — Errors and refusal: Say what happened, next to the thing.
- `/irisui/patterns/onboarding.md` — Onboarding and permission: Ask once, say what for.
- `/irisui/patterns/talk.md` — Talking and voice: Listening, thinking, talking, muted.
